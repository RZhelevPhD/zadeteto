# Competitor Watch List — ZaDeteto

> **Scope (narrow, deliberate):** sites that sell **paid placement / paid listings / paid visibility** to Bulgarian children's-services specialists. These compete directly with ZaDeteto's **partner tier** for the specialist's marketing wallet. A specialist evaluating "where do I spend my €49/mo for visibility?" sees these platforms in the same decision.
>
> **Key observation from the field:** most of these competitors pitch specialists via cold email with weak, unprofessional offers (no differentiation, no quality gate, no audit, vague traffic promises). That is itself a strategic opportunity — ZaDeteto's partner acquisition should *feel* different from their pitch in voice, specificity, and rigor.
>
> **Read this file when:**
> - Auditing partner-side copy (partners.html, pricing.html, outreach templates) — so we don't mirror a competitor's framing or concede differentiation
> - Before outreach to a specialist prospect — so we know which paid-placement pitches have probably already hit their inbox and what to contrast against
> - Setting or defending partner pricing — so we know where we sit in the specialist's budget comparison
> - Designing objection handling — "I already pay for X" is the most likely objection and X is probably in this file
>
> **Privacy:** Internal reference. Do not publish.

## How to use this file

### For AHDM
- Before a **partners.html / pricing.html / partner-facing copy** audit: grep competitor entries for the pricing bands, guarantee language, and "what you get" lists they use. Ensure ZaDeteto's offer stack does NOT verbally overlap in vague territory (e.g. generic "повече видимост", "повече клиенти") and has a clear contrast on audit / verification / capacity.
- Before a **hero / positioning** audit: scan the "Do NOT accidentally copy" line of each entry.
- Before a **proof / trust-signal** audit: note which competitors display review counts / badges / stars and how gameable they look — ZaDeteto's proof must be *qualitatively different*, not just *more*.

### For BD
- Before outreach to a specialist who may already be paying a competitor: **run the "I already pay for X" objection playbook** (see BD's BC3 Inbound M8 "How To Respond" + BC2 M13 "Future State") — but ground the response in the specific competitor's weakness from this file. Generic objection handling loses; specific objection handling ("yes, and here's the concrete difference between what they give you and what you'd get from a ZaDeteto audit") wins.
- When a prospect pushes back on price, read the "Price point" line of the relevant competitor — a specialist paying €X/mo for a free-text listing elsewhere gives you the framework for the value comparison.

### For prospect-research
When building a dossier, Grep this file for the prospect's name and company. If the prospect is listed on any tracked competitor, add a **"Competitor entanglements"** line to the dossier showing which ones, which tier, and the likely objection ("they already pay logopedia.bg for X / they are a premium listing on Y").

# Adjacent competitors

## logopedia.bg — ЛОГОПЕДИЯ БГ

- **URL:** https://logopedia.bg/ — specialist list: https://logopedia.bg/index.php?option=com_comprofiler&task=userslist&Itemid=117
- **Tier:** **Adjacent** (free listing → does not compete for the partner wallet) + **Authority** (award-giving body)
- **Why Adjacent, not Direct:** the directory listing is free. Specialists don't choose between "pay logopedia.bg" and "pay ZaDeteto." However, they DO compete for the specialist's **attention** and **reputational allegiance**, and the award function makes them an Authority player.
- **Entity:** Taktix Group Ltd, est. 2012
- **Last verified:** 2026-04-18

### Monetization model
- Directory listing: **free**
- Revenue: paid conferences / trainings (e.g. "Приложна Логопедия", summer psychomotor training)
- No tiered placement, no premium badge, no verification fee

### Directory mechanics
- 836 registered therapists as of 2026-04-18
- Listings show name + city only — no specialty, credentials, contact, or profile-completion indicator
- Search: name field only (no city filter, no specialty filter)
- No verification, no tier, no capacity limit, no badge
- Listings visually undifferentiated → Partner Pain Map #6 Commoditization Anxiety environment

### Known historical actions
- **2023-04:** revoked Александър Кърджев's "Speech Therapist of the Year" award after a photo-association with an unrelated controversy. He returned the award, publicly disputed the implication. Source: marica.bg, 2023-04-07. This matters for any BD outreach to Kardjev or anyone who was watching this episode.

### Relevance to ZaDeteto's partner tier decision
- **Low direct overlap** on budget — free service doesn't take wallet share.
- **Medium indirect overlap** on narrative — when a speech therapist thinks "where should I be visible online?", logopedia.bg is on the list even though it's free.
- **Conversion opportunity:** those 836 undifferentiated names are a prospect pool. The partner pitch writes itself: *"You're currently 1 of 836 names on a list with no filters. Parents can't find you there — they can only find your name."*

### Pivot risk
Low-but-non-zero: if they add paid tiers / verification in the next 12 months, they become Direct overnight. 14 years of inaction suggests it won't happen, but monitor.

### Do NOT accidentally copy
- Their "портал, който обединява..." generic-portal framing. Our copy must be sharper.
- Their undifferentiated audience framing ("за родители, за специалисти, за всички"). We keep two clearly split sides.

### Monitor
- Any pricing page
- Any "verified" / "проверен" / "акредитиран" / tier language
- Any new filters on the directory
- Any standards / audit statements
- Award/scandal news
