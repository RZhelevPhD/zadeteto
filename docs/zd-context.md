## Mission

Да създадем доверена среда, в която всеки родител може да намери проверен, качествен специалист за детето си — без догадки, без Facebook съвети от непознати, без страх от грешен избор.

**English:** Create a trusted environment where every parent can find a verified, quality specialist for their child — without guessing, without Facebook advice from strangers, without fear of making the wrong choice.

---

## Vision

Да станем националният стандарт за качество при детските услуги в България. Платформа, при която "ЗаДетето потвърден" означава нещо — така, както Michelin звезда означава нещо в ресторантьорството.

**English:** Become the national quality standard for children's services in Bulgaria. A platform where "ZaDeteto verified" means something — the way a Michelin star means something in restaurants.

---

## Values

1. **Доверие преди всичко** (Trust Above All) — Every decision filtered through "does this increase trust?"
2. **Качество над количество** (Quality Over Quantity) — Limited capacity per city, strict auditing, high barrier to entry
3. **Детето е на първо място** (The Child Comes First) — All standards, audits, and decisions oriented toward child safety and wellbeing
4. **Прозрачност** (Transparency) — Clear pricing, clear standards, clear expectations for both parents and partners
5. **Антиаматьорство** (Anti-Amateurism) — We exist because amateurs can call themselves "specialists" with no consequences. We refuse to let that stand.

---

## What ZaDeteto Is

A **national verified directory for children's specialists and services** in Bulgaria. Two-sided marketplace:

- **Parents** search for free → find verified specialists → make confident choices
- **Specialists/Organizations** pay for verified listings → get qualified parent traffic → build professional reputation

**Launch date:** June 1, 2026
**Geography:** Bulgaria-wide (Sofia, Plovdiv, Varna, Burgas, and all other cities)

## Who We Serve

### Parents (Consumer Side — Free)

**Avatar:** Bulgarian parent (predominantly mothers, 25-45) looking for quality children's services.

**Research Data:**
- 91.4% от родителите не вярват, че институциите могат да им помогнат и се съмняват в професионализма на специалистите
- 61.1% плащат повече, когато не са сигурни на кого да се доверят
- 72.7% търсят стойност и качество, а не просто по-ниска цена

### Parent Pain Map (Structured)

**1. Epistemic Uncertainty (Can't Verify)**
- "Всеки може да се нарече специалист във фейсбук. Без доказателства, без критерии, без отговорност. (И без последици.)"
- "Търсиш в Google и попадаш на табели, не на опит."
- No way to verify credentials, training, or actual competence
- Reviews are gameable and unreliable

**2. Information Chaos (Too Much Noise)**
- "Ровиш във форуми. Питаш в 5 Facebook групи. Получаваш 47 коментара."
- "Три или четири са полезни, но твърде общи. Пет си противоречат."
- "Всяка противоречива препоръка храни съмнението."
- Signal-to-noise ratio is terrible — quality providers drowned by ads and amateurs

**3. Financial Risk (Overpaying for Uncertainty)**
- 61.1% overpay when uncertain who to trust
- Parents compare by price because they have no other quality signal
- "Родителите, които търсят качество, го разпознават трудно и често сравняват само по цена."

**4. Decision Paralysis & Delayed Action**
- "Несигурният родител или избира прибързано, или отлага. Поне докато стане твърде късно."
- Two failure modes: rushing into a bad choice OR postponing and missing the developmental window

**5. Emotional Stakes (It's About Their Child)**
- "А когато става дума за деца, рискът не е безобиден."
- This isn't buying a product — it's choosing someone who will directly impact their child's development, safety, and wellbeing
- Guilt and anxiety amplify every decision

**6. Time Cost (Research Burden)**
- Hours spent researching, asking, cross-referencing — with no guarantee of a good outcome
- Every minute spent searching is a minute not spent with their child

### Parent Problem Stack (StoryBrand)

**External Problem:** Can't verify credentials, quality, or safety of children's service providers
**Internal Problem:** "Чувствам се несигурен/на и претоварен/а от избора" — Overwhelmed and insecure about choosing
**Philosophical Problem:** Parents shouldn't have to gamble with their children's wellbeing

**Villain:** The unregulated marketplace where anyone can call themselves a specialist with zero accountability

**Transformation:** От „надявам се да не сгреша" до „знам, че избрах правилния"
("From 'I hope I don't make a mistake' to 'I know I chose the right one'")

### Specialists & Organizations (B2B — Paying)

**Avatar:** Quality-first Bulgarian specialists and organizations working with children who are tired of being lumped in with amateurs.

**Categories:**
- Детски психолози и логопеди (Child psychologists & speech therapists)
- Образователни школи (Educational schools)
- Специалисти по ранно развитие (Early development)
- Организатори на забавления (Entertainment organizers)
- Търговци на стоки (Children's products)
- Домашни грижи и детегледачи (Home care & nannies)
- Култура и изкуство (Culture & arts)
- Спорт (Sports)
- Събития (Events)

### Partner Pain Map (Structured)

**1. Visibility Crisis (Invisible Behind Noise)**
- "В морето от реклами опита Ви остава невидим."
- "Шумът затрупва качествените специалисти. Те остават невидими зад шарени реклами."
- Quality work doesn't market itself — amateurs with louder ads win the attention game
- Years of training and experience mean nothing if parents can't find you

**2. Race-to-Bottom Pricing Pressure**
- "Родителите, които търсят качество, го разпознават трудно и често сравняват само по цена. Останалите просто търсят евтиното."
- When trust signals don't exist, price becomes the only comparison point
- Quality providers forced to discount to compete with amateurs charging less

**3. Inadequate Compensation for Expertise**
- "Ако не се отделите от шума, рискувате да останете най-добре пазената тайна в бранша и да продължавате да НЕ получавате заплащане, което е достойно за вложения труд."
- Experts doing deeply skilled work being paid as if they're interchangeable with amateurs
- Can't charge premium rates without a way to prove premium quality

**4. Market Amateurization (No Standards)**
- "Всеки може да се нарече специалист във фейсбук. Без доказателства, без критерии, без отговорност."
- No professional filtering or credentialing system exists for children's services in Bulgaria
- The market doesn't distinguish between a 15-year clinical expert and someone who took a weekend course

**5. Client Acquisition Friction**
- Spending marketing budget with no proof of ROI
- Facebook/Google reviews gameable and unreliable as trust signals
- Word-of-mouth is slow and unscalable
- No centralized place where qualified parents are actively looking

**6. Commoditization Anxiety**
- Fear that their specialized expertise is being reduced to a commodity
- Competing on the same platforms as people who have no business calling themselves specialists
- Professional identity erosion

### Partner Problem Stack (StoryBrand)

**External Problem:** No credible verification system exists for children's services in Bulgaria
**Internal Problem:** "Знам, че съм добър/а в това, което правя, но не мога да го докажа на нови клиенти" — Know they're good but can't prove it to new clients
**Philosophical Problem:** Real professionals shouldn't have to compete with amateurs on equal footing

**Villain:** The unfiltered marketplace that treats every self-proclaimed "specialist" the same

**Transformation:** From invisible quality practitioner → recognized, verified expert with consistent, qualified parent flow

## Pricing & Offer Mechanics

### Tier Structure (Founder's Round — locked forever for early adopters)

| Tier | Price | Audit Fee | Key Features | Badge |
|------|-------|-----------|-------------|-------|
| **Стандартен** | €95 one-time + €49/yr | None | Basic listing, 1 category, 2 tags, 4 contact icons | Blue "Стандартен" |
| **Проверен** | €29/mo or €348/yr | €147 (or included in yearly) | Full profile, description, 1 CTA, lead magnets, verified badge, audit score | Green "Проверен" |
| **Доверен** | €49/mo or €588/yr | €197 (or included in yearly) | SEO controls, booking calendar, product shop, 2 CTAs, priority placement | Purple "Доверен" |
| **Премиум** | Custom | Custom | Unlimited, custom branding, dedicated manager, monthly reports | Gold "Премиум" |

### Key Offer Mechanics

- **Auditing as barrier to entry** — Not a purchase; a quality gate. Only passed audits grant tier status.
- **Capacity limits** — Sofia: 20 positions/category. Plovdiv/Varna/Burgas: 10. Other cities: 5. Creates scarcity and ensures quality lead flow to each partner.
- **Founder's Round pricing lock** — Pre-June 1 partners lock pricing forever. Post-launch prices will be higher.
- **Yearly recertification** — Audit isn't one-time; partners must re-certify annually.
- **"We optimize if no traction" guarantee** — Platform commits to personally optimize partner presence if they're not getting results.

### Revenue Model

1. Monthly/annual partner subscriptions (primary)
2. One-time audit fees (setup)
3. Annual recertification (recurring)

Parents use the platform **free**. All revenue comes from the B2B side.

## Competitive Positioning

### What We're NOT
- Not a generic directory (like Yellow Pages)
- Not an unmoderated marketplace (like Facebook groups)
- Not a review platform (like Yelp — reviews are supplementary, not the trust signal)
- Not pay-to-play (paying doesn't make you verified — passing the audit does)

### Our Moat
1. **Expert auditing** — Founders have 15+ years clinical experience in child development
2. **Limited capacity** — Supply curation = quality guarantee for both sides
3. **Network effects** — More parents → more value for partners; more quality partners → more parents
4. **Reputation** — Failed audits = public credibility risk; incentivizes maintenance of high standards

### Competitive Tagline
"Проверената общност за специалисти, които отказват да се конкурират с аматьорите"
("The verified community for specialists who refuse to compete with amateurs")
